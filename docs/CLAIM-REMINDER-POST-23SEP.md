## The one fact that matters

Every migration mint matures on **30 September** — day 30 of the chain. If
you claim before then, your staked LASSECASH earns yield and votes from the
moment you claim until maturity. If you claim after, you get every token,
but the earning month is over.

- **Claim now → your stake earns and votes until 30 Sep.**
- Claim 30 Sep – 29 Dec (grace) → full amount, straight to liquid, no yield.
- Claim 29 Dec – 29 Mar 2027 (bleed) → the surviving fraction shrinks
  linearly to zero; what bleeds recycles to the reward pool.
- After 29 Mar 2027 → claiming closes; unclaimed positions are swept to the
  reward pool. Seven months of window in total — but only the first month
  earns.

Claiming costs nothing — a fresh Hive account's free Resource Credits cover
it. Sign in at **lassecash.com**, the claim box is at the top of the Mint
page, and every figure is previewed before you sign anything.
