About LasseCash
Overview
LasseCash is a decentralized cryptocurrency and social media platform built as a historic "outpost" on Hive Engine, operating on foundational ideas by Lasse Ehlers with primary graphics design by Carlos Serrano (@zaxan). Launched on June 28, 2019, the ecosystem is designed to reward anarchy, cryptocurrency, and truth-focused content through zero-fee, rapid transactions.

Key Features & Tokenomics
Green and Fast Transactions: Operates on Green Day 1 principles with 3-second transactions and zero fees, contrasting sharply with high-energy blockchains like Bitcoin.

Proof of Brain Rewards: Users earn LASSECASH and LASSECASH POWER by creating blog posts. Payout ratios are uniquely structured at 90% Lassecash Power and 10% Lassecash, incentivizing long-term participation with a power-down schedule spanning 26 payments every 7 days over 6 months.

Extended Utility: The platform allows users to utilize up to 20 tags per blog post (compared to 8-10 on competing sites) completely ad-free.

Diesel Pool Rewards: Liquidity providers for the SWAP.HIVE:LASSECASH pool earn daily rewards featuring a compounding loyalty bonus of an extra 1% per day up to 30 days, capping at a 30% extra daily reward for long-term providers.

NFT Platform: Supports minting and trading image, audio, and video NFTs focused primarily on anarchy, crypto, and truth themes.

Token Supply & Distribution
Hard Cap: Maximum of 51 million tokens ever issued.

Circulating Metrics: Featuring a circulating supply structure that includes pool contracts and inflation tokens, with Lasse Ehlers holding a transparent ~23% of the true circulating supply.

Dynamic Inflation: Inflation is managed transparently via blockchain announcements, with upcoming schedules leading into the planned MAGI migration where inflation will be set in stone with a halving mechanism.

Future Development & MAGI Migration
LasseCash is preparing for a migration to the MAGI blockchain. This transition aims to eliminate the limitations of Hive Engine, establish fixed-schedule tokenomics with halving mechanics, and open up opportunities for zero-fee decentralized finance tools designed for an anarcho-capitalist framework.
