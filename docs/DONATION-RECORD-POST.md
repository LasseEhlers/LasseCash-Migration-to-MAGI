# Six years of giving LASSECASH away, one signature at a time

*Draft for Lasse. Every number below is from Hive-Engine's own history for
@lasseehlers and can be reproduced by anyone. Transfers to my own accounts are
excluded — only gifts to other people are counted.*

---

Since May 2020 I have sent LASSECASH to strangers by hand. Not through a
distribution contract, not through a bot, not as an airdrop I set up once and
forgot. One transaction at a time, chosen one person at a time.

Here is the whole record.

```
592 separate gifts
275 different people
12,750,853.98 LASSECASH
2020-05-14  →  2026-08-17
```

**I hold 7,268,389 LASSECASH today. I have given away more than I own.**

I was allocated 11 million at inception in 2019. Everything else that exists
came from the inflation schedule I published before day one and never touched
for myself. The difference between 11 million and 7.27 million went to other
people, and so did the 12.75 million above.

## Amsterdam, 26 September 2022

HiveFest⁷ ran 22–25 September 2022 in Amsterdam. I paid for the flight, the
hotel — an expensive very small room — and the conference ticket, which I got
on the largest discount tier because I had very little Hive Power. I spoke on
stage about LasseCash for free.

Afterwards I walked around the venue asking people if they wanted some. When
somebody said yes I wrote their username on a paper pad and put a number beside
it, based on nothing but how the conversation had gone.

The day after it ended, I went home and sent the pad.

```

             @iliyan90       100,000

                            ─────────

```

Nine people, one afternoon, 55% of everything I gave away that entire year.
All of it liquid, not staked — these were handshakes, not onboarding.

Six of the nine went on to do something with it. Two became real users for a
while — one staked twenty-nine times and earned fifty-two payouts, another
earned thirty-six and holds more today than I gave her. Three never touched it.

For a handshake at a conference, that is a better rate than I expected when I
was writing names on a pad.

## Why

Most of these people never asked for anything. Someone would leave one good
comment on a post and get 10,000 LASSECASH an hour later, for a reason nobody
ever explained to them.

I do not regret any of it. I built something for seven years while nearly
everyone assumed it was nothing, and giving it away to strangers across the
world in three seconds, for free, with no permission needed from anyone, was
the part that always felt like proof the thing worked.

## Today

Today I did it once more, and for the first time with a script: **9.00292595
LASSECASH each to 4,443 active Hive accounts that have never used LasseCash**,
with a letter attached to every one.

**That batch is deliberately not counted in the numbers above.** It is a
different act — mass, tiny, automated — and mixing it into a hand-built record
would cheapen both. The 275 stand on their own.
