# LasseCash video kit — intro, outro, thumbnails

Everything an editor needs to make a video open and close in the site's own
identity. Generated by `generate.py` from the brand sources (the mark SVGs in
`../logo/`, the colours in `web/src/app.css`); re-run it after any brand
change, never edit the PNGs.

**Scope: the LasseCash channel (@LasseCashNews) and videos about LasseCash.
Lasse Music has its own branding.**

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `lassecash-mark-2048.png` | the square mark, flat, transparent — the hero for an animated reveal |
| `lassecash-mark-glow-2048.png` | the same with the header's gold glow — for dark backgrounds, which every LasseCash frame is |
| `wordmark-1920.png` · `wordmark-3840.png` | LASSECASH / ANCAP SOCIETY TOOLS, transparent, for 1080p and 4K |
| `lockup-1920.png` | mark + wordmark in a row, exactly as the site header lays it out |
| `title-card-1920x1080.png` | a finished intro/end frame: grid, glow mark, wordmark, lassecash.com |
| `../logo/lassecash-mark.svg` | the vector, for any tool that animates paths (After Effects, Blender, Motion) |

## The numbers, so nothing drifts

- Gold **#ffd23f** (`--gold`) · background **#07090d** (`--bg`) · the cream of "LASSE" **#f3e6c4** (sampled from the share card) · secondary text grey #8c8c96
- Font: **JetBrains Mono** — ExtraBold for LASSECASH, Bold for the tagline with **0.18em** tracking (the header's), Bold for URLs
- The mark's own proportions are in the SVG; do not stretch it, do not add a stroke, do not put it on white in a video
- Glow is `--glow-gold`, the header's; the glow PNG already has it baked in

## A three-beat intro that matches the site (suggestion, not a rule)

1. **0.0–0.8s** — black (`#07090d`) with the faint grid; the mark fades/scales in with its glow, centred.
2. **0.8–1.6s** — the mark slides left as LASSECASH types or wipes in to its right (the lockup).
3. **1.6–2.5s** — ANCAP SOCIETY TOOLS tracks in beneath; hold, then cut to the content.

Outro: the title card, still, with the URL — long enough to read it, 3–4s.
Keep the whole intro under three seconds; it runs before every video.
